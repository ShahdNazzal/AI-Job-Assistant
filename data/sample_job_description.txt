Job Title: Junior AI Engineer

About the role
We are looking for a motivated junior AI engineer to join our applied AI team.
You will help design, build and deploy machine-learning features for our
customer-facing products, working closely with senior engineers and product
managers.

Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain machine learning models for text classification and
  semantic search.
- Build data processing pipelines with Python and SQL.
- Serve models through REST APIs and monitor their performance.
- Collaborate on code reviews, testing and documentation.
- Help evaluate and integrate LLM-based features such as retrieval-augmented
  generation.

Required qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field.
- 1+ years of experience with Python for data or machine learning work
  (projects and internships count).
- Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals, including model
  evaluation and cross-validation.
- Experience with scikit-learn and at least one deep learning framework
  such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Working knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
- Familiarity with Git-based collaboration.
- Basic experience with REST API development in Python (e.g. FastAPI or Flask).

Preferred qualifications
- Exposure to NLP techniques: embeddings, transformers, Hugging Face
  Transformers library.
- Knowledge of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) concepts and vector
  databases such as FAISS or pgvector.
- Experience with Docker and CI/CD workflows.
- Familiarity with AWS services (S3, Lambda) or another cloud platform.
- Knowledge of MLOps practices such as experiment tracking and model
  deployment.

Benefits
- Competitive salary and equity.
- Annual learning budget and conference support.
- Hybrid work policy based in our Seattle office.
